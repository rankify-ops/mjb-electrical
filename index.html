FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our residential electrical services.

What residential services do you offer?

We cover the full range of home electrical work — switchboard upgrades and safety inspections, power points, lighting and ceiling fans, new home wiring and renovation fit-outs, fault finding, smoke alarm installation, split system air conditioning, and ongoing maintenance and repairs.

Do you offer a free on-site quote?

Yes. A licensed electrician visits your property, assesses the job and answers your questions — up to one hour, completely free and with no obligation. There is no call-out fee for a quote.

Do you upgrade old switchboards?

We do. Switchboard upgrades are one of our most common jobs — modern safety upgrades with RCD protection, tidy wiring and full compliance checks so your property meets current Victorian standards.

Can you install split system air conditioning?

Yes — we handle split system installation including the electrical supply and circuit work, so the job is completed properly and to compliance in one visit.

Do you handle smoke alarm compliance?

We install, replace and test smoke alarms for homes and rental properties, making sure your alarms meet Victorian compliance requirements.
